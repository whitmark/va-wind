2 · Yorktown Beach (Water St, Yorktown)

No Yorktown State Park exists; this is Yorktown Beach — York County's public beach on Water St along the Riverwalk, just downriver (SE) of the Coleman Bridge.
Beach center 37.2364, -76.5058 · faces ~49° NE (shoreline measured from Esri imagery, ±10°) · forecast target: open river 37.2388, -76.5024 (0.4 km offshore) · tides: NOAA Yorktown USCG 8637689 (harmonic, 6-min predictions) · current: NOAA ACT5436 Yorktown (by the Coleman Bridge)
With this facing: N and E are cross-onshore (N best, E good per Mark), NE dead onshore, NW cross-shore (good), W cross-off (maybe), S/SW blow off the land (no).
Local windsurf blog: best in E/NE on an outgoing (ebb) tide · strongest currents are near the Coleman Bridge; wind against current gets rough, stay down-current of the bridge · parking is free: Riverwalk Landing Parking Terrace, 330 Water St (lower level 3-h limit, upper deck unlimited) and county lots (York Hall, Courthouse, Admin Bldg) — no grass/side-street parking · beach rules: no dogs, alcohol, glass, grills, bonfires; no climbing/fishing on the rocks; Apr 1–Oct 31 groups of 10+ need a permit 10 AM–4 PM weekends; no posted rule on windsurfing found, so give swimmers a wide berth (it is a swimming beach) · rock breakwaters / pocket beaches along the shore · busy boat traffic, the shipping channel and Navy traffic (Naval Weapons Station Yorktown, Coast Guard Training Center) are close offshore · Water 64.2°F measured at NOAA Yorktown USCG 8637689, as of Fri 6 Nov 2026 07:24 ET
